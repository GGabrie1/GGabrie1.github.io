01 — About Me

A curious engineer with a builder's mindset

I enjoy problems that sit between disciplines — where control theory meets machine learning, and where code has to survive contact with real hardware.

I'm a final-year BEng (Hons) Electrical and Electronic Engineering student at the University of Nottingham Ningbo China, currently maintaining a GPA of 3.86/4.00. My interests span autonomous systems, robot perception and control, embedded design and applied machine learning.

Over the past two years I have worked across six engineering projects, from a 50 W two-switch forward converter and a radar speed detector, to an autonomous driving car and a BLE 5.1 real-time location system. My current research focuses on real-time ship trajectory forecasting, where I combine Transformer-based models with LLM reasoning to predict and explain vessel behaviour.

I care about engineering that works outside the lab: simulations are a tool, not the goal. That's why I like to move from model to measurement — PCB, firmware, and data — as quickly as possible.
