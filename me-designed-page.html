Two-Switch Forward Converter

Team leader · power electronics design

50 W, 10 V, 100 kHz isolated converter with a two-layer PCB, hand-wound magnetics and a Type III compensated UC3524A loop.

83.5 mV ripple−80 % vs uncompensated50 W / 10 V
Technical detail
  • Designed the two-layer PCB in KiCad and hand-wound high-frequency magnetics optimised for skin effect and saturation.
